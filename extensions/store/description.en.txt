Send a problem's sample tests to the C++ Here online editor with one click. No more copying and pasting.

WHAT IT DOES
Open a problem on an online judge and click the toolbar button (or press Ctrl+Shift+U, ⌘+Shift+U on Mac). The editor opens with every example input and output already loaded as a test case. Write your solution, press "Run All", and each one runs on its own and is checked against the expected answer. Passing ones turn green.

WHY INSTALL IT
• Save time: three or four examples mean six to eight copy-and-paste trips. Now it is one click.
• No pasting mistakes: everything is read straight from the page, down to the trailing newline.
• Works on more than 100 websites, including Codeforces, AtCoder and CSES. The full list is on GitHub (link below).
• Works for contests too: on a supported contest page, import the whole contest at once.
• No compiler to install: you write, run and check your code right in the browser.

MORE FEATURES
• A tab that is already open is reused instead of opening a new one. If it already has test cases, you are asked whether to replace them or add the new ones.
• On a page that isn't recognized automatically, right-click the toolbar button and choose from the "Parse with" menu.
• Custom rules in the options let you always use a specific parser for URLs that match a pattern.

PRIVACY
• The extension reads a page only when you click its button, press its shortcut, or choose from its menu.
• It contains no analytics or tracking code, and it never reads your browsing history, form fields, passwords or cookies.
• What it reads goes only to the editor tab in your browser. The extension does not send it to any server.
